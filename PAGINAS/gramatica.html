Present simple — statements

Use the present simple for things that are generally true or always happen. Add -s for he/she/it.

I / You / We / TheyHe / She / It
I live in a flat.She lives in a house.
Present continuous — statements & Where's...?

Use the present continuous for things happening right now. Form: am/is/are + verb-ing.

StatementQuestion with Where's...?
Daisy is walking to school.Where's Daisy's house? — It's next to the park.
be good at
be good at + nounMy parents are good at English. · She isn't good at sport.
be good at + verb + -ingThat boy is good at dancing. · They aren't good at fishing.

OfferAnswer

Example
